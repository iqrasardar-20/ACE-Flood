# Limitations and experiments not completed

- 4 humid eastern-US basins and one test year (63 flood-days); no regional, national or global claim.
- Hindcast with retrospective gridded meteorology and approved discharge; no rainfall forecasts.
- Full CAMELS-US (671 basins) and multiple test years not run (archive not obtainable in the study environment).
- The miss-rate target is an empirical property here, not a guarantee; the online bound is vacuous at this sample size.
- The flood threshold (long-term Q95, 1989–2009) overlaps the test year; a training-only threshold is reported as a sensitivity.
- Simulated gaps are missing at random; the natural gap pattern hides no test flood-day.
- No paired tests for the threshold-definition and missing-data experiments.
- Several hyperparameters sit at the edge of their grids; tuning at h = 1 only.
